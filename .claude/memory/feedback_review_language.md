## Review Agent Language — Positive, Not Negative

When dispatching review agents, frame the stakes positively:

**Wrong:** "Do NOT rubber-stamp. If everything is perfect, explain WHY."

**Right:** "You are reviewing instructions for a Senior Living App Framework. Any lack of focus, guessing, or critical thinking will cost dearly. Root yourself in pragmatic excellence."

**Why:** Negative framing ("don't do X") tells Claude what to avoid but not what standard to achieve. Positive framing sets the bar: pragmatic excellence, stakes-aware, focused. Claude 4.6 responds better to motivation + standard than to prohibition.

**How to apply:** Every review agent prompt should open with:
- What the system is (senior living framework)
- What the stakes are (residents depend on it)
- What standard to meet (pragmatic excellence)
- NOT what to avoid (no "don't", "never", "do not")

Also applies to the process-flows documents themselves — rewrite prohibitions as standards.
